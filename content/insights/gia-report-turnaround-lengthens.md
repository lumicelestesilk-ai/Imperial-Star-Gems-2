**Standard GIA turnaround on natural diamond reports has stretched by several business days as festive-season submissions climb.**

- Submission volumes to GIA typically rise heading into the autumn wedding and festive season, and this year is no exception.
- Standard-service turnaround has lengthened by roughly a week compared with the summer, though express service remains close to normal.
- Lab-grown submissions, mostly going to IGI, have not seen the same slowdown.

If a natural stone is being bought loose for a report-in-hand purchase, build in extra time before a deadline, or ask us for stones already carrying a current report. [See natural stones in stock with reports on hand](/natural-diamonds). The [grading report](/glossary/grading-report) glossary entry explains what standard versus express service actually changes.
