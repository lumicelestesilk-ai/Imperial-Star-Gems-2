**Claims patterns we've seen suggest loose stones travelling by insured courier see fewer loss incidents than the same value carried personally, despite the instinct to hand-carry valuables.**

- Personal carrying feels safer but concentrates risk on a single person and route, with no tracking and no institutional recovery process if something goes wrong.
- Insured courier services track a parcel at every stage and carry underwritten cover specifically for high-value goods in transit.
- The exception is very short, local transfers, where personal handover with a signed receipt can be simpler and just as safe as arranging a courier.
