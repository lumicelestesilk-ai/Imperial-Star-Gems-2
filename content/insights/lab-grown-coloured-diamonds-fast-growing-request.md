**Lab-grown pink and blue diamonds, produced with post-growth treatment, are a small but fast-growing request category, offering fancy colour at a fraction of natural fancy colour pricing.**

- Most lab-grown fancy colour stones reach their final colour through irradiation and annealing after growth, a treatment that must be disclosed on the grading report.
- Pricing sits well below natural fancy colour stones of comparable colour and saturation, which is the main draw for buyers choosing lab-grown here.
- Buyers should confirm treatment disclosure explicitly, distinct from the [broader push on post-growth treatment disclosure](/insights/post-growth-treatment-disclosure-standards-are-tightening-for-cvd) for colourless CVD stones.
