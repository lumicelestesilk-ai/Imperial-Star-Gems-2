**Small natural melee has stopped falling, after roughly two years in which lab-grown substitution pulled prices steadily down.**

- Melee under 0.18 ct, the small stones set as pavé and accents rather than sold as centre stones, fell for two years as jewellery manufacturers shifted pavé work to lab-grown.
- That substitution has largely run its course: most volume manufacturers already buy lab-grown melee by default.
- Natural melee asking prices have held flat for the past two months, the longest stretch without a decline since the shift began.

The floor appears to be manufacturers who specifically market a piece as all-natural and price accordingly.
