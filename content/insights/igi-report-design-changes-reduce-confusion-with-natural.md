**Updated report formatting is intended to make lab-grown reports more visually distinct from natural diamond reports at a glance, addressing confusion that has occasionally come up between the two.**

- Older report designs sometimes required reading the fine print to confirm whether a stone was natural or laboratory-grown, which created room for accidental or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Newer formats use clearer heading text and, in some cases, distinct colour coding to flag lab-grown status immediately rather than requiring close reading.
- This complements physical laser inscription, which already marks most lab-grown stones on the girdle as laboratory-grown alongside the report number.

We check report format and girdle inscription on every lab-grown stone before it's listed, regardless of which lab graded it. [See our grading standards](/spec-sheet/lab-grown) for details.
