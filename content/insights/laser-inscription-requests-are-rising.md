**More buyers are asking for the grading report number to be laser-inscribed on the stone's girdle, linking a specific stone to its paperwork in a way that can't be swapped out.**

- Inscription is a microscopic engraving on the girdle, invisible without magnification, that carries the report number and sometimes a short personal message.
- Interest has risen alongside the broader push toward stronger documentation we noted with [provenance paperwork](/insights/provenance-documentation-requests-are-becoming-the-norm) — an inscription is a physical version of the same idea, harder to separate from the stone than a certificate can be.
- Buyers who plan to insure, resell or pass down a stone are the most likely to ask for it, since it removes any doubt about which certificate belongs to which stone.

Inscription is a quick, low-cost addition at the time of grading and cannot easily be added later without a stone going back to a lab. If it matters to you, ask for it when you order rather than after — [contact us](/contact) and we can arrange it on any stone we supply.
