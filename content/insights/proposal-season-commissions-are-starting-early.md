**Bespoke engagement ring enquiries that usually cluster around the December-to-February proposal season have started arriving since early September this year.**

- Custom build enquiries are running ahead of where they sat at the same point last year, based on our own order pipeline.
- Buyers starting earlier get first pick of well-matched stones and more realistic turnaround on a custom setting, rather than competing for the same inventory in the final weeks before the holidays.
- The earlier start lines up with [tighter cutting capacity moving into Q4](/insights/surat-cutting-capacity-tightens-into-q4), which rewards ordering ahead rather than waiting.

If a proposal is planned for the coming season, starting the search now rather than in November gives more choice on shape, size and setting. Our [build-a-ring](/build-a-ring) tool is a good starting point, and the [trade desk](/contact) can advise on sourcing a specific stone to a timeline.
