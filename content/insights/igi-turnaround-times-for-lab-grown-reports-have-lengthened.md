**Standard grading turnaround at IGI, which handles most lab-grown volume, has stretched as submissions keep climbing.**

- IGI has become the primary lab for lab-grown reports as volumes have grown, a shift we [covered when it overtook GIA in lab-grown share](/insights/igi-volumes-overtake-gia-in-lab-grown).
- That same growth is now showing up as longer queues, echoing the [turnaround delays already seen at GIA for natural stones](/insights/gia-report-turnaround-lengthens), though from a faster starting point.
- Express services remain available at a premium and are largely unaffected, so the delay mainly affects standard-service submissions on lower-value stones.

If a lab-grown purchase is time-sensitive, ask whether the stone already carries a report or would need fresh grading before it can ship — buying already-certified stock avoids the wait entirely.
