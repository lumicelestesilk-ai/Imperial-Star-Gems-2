**The price difference between natural and lab-grown grows with size, and at the larger end it is still growing.**

- Lab-grown 2 ct wholesale prices fell about a fifth over the year to the second quarter.
- Larger natural stones held broadly steady through the summer.
- At a carat, both origins have steadied, so the gap there is holding roughly where it is.

For a buyer choosing on size within a set budget, lab-grown at 2 ct and above now goes furthest; natural keeps its premium most strongly at the same sizes.
