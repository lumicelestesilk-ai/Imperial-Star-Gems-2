**Enquiries specifically asking about previously-owned natural stones, rather than new goods, have grown as a share of the buying we see.**

- Estate stones — natural diamonds bought back from a previous owner or an inherited piece, re-graded and re-sold — have always circulated in the trade, but buyer-initiated requests for them have picked up this year.
- The appeal is straightforward: an older, well-cut natural stone at a larger size can cost less than a new stone of the same specification, particularly since [wholesale-to-retail spreads have narrowed](/insights/wholesale-to-retail-spreads-are-narrowing-on-natural-stones) on new goods.
- Older cuts also carry proportions that are harder to find in new production, which is part of what is driving interest in [antique cushion and old European cuts](/insights/antique-cushion-and-old-european-cuts-in-demand) specifically.

An estate stone still needs a current grading report before purchase, since an old certificate may predate today's standards or simply be out of date. If you are considering a previously-owned stone, [ask us](/contact) to check current documentation before you buy, whether or not the stone comes from us.
