**New CVD reactor capacity coming online continues to outpace near-term demand growth, the underlying imbalance behind [lab-grown prices still falling](/insights/lab-grown-still-falling-more-slowly), even if more slowly than before.**

- Reactor investment decisions made a few years ago, during a period of stronger demand growth, are still coming online now, adding supply into a more moderate demand environment.
- Some growers have slowed or paused new capacity additions in response, which is part of why the rate of price decline has itself slowed.
- A genuine rebalancing would likely require either a period of little new capacity or a fresh acceleration in demand, and there's no clear sign of either yet.
