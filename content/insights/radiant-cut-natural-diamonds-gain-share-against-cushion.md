**Among fancy shapes, radiant cuts have picked up a growing share of enquiries at the expense of the classic cushion, which has dominated fancy-shape requests for years.**

- A radiant cut combines a rectangular or square outline with cropped corners and a brilliant facet pattern, giving more fire than a cushion of similar proportions.
- The shift seems tied to a broader move toward more angular, geometric settings in custom ring design, which suits a radiant's crisper outline.
- Radiant cuts are also somewhat more efficient to cut from certain rough shapes than cushions, which has kept pricing competitive as demand has grown.
