**Wealth and investment commentary is starting to cite polished diamond price indices alongside gold and silver, though the two behave very differently as assets.**

- Gold and silver trade on transparent, continuously quoted spot markets; diamond price indices are built from a sample of wholesale transactions and lag real-time pricing.
- Diamonds are not fungible the way bullion is — two stones of the same carat weight can differ in value by a large margin depending on cut, colour and clarity.
- Treating a diamond index the way you'd treat a spot gold price risks overstating how liquid or comparable individual stones actually are.

A diamond can be a good store of value in a specific stone bought well, but it isn't a substitute for a liquid commodity market. [Ask the trade desk](/contact) before treating any index figure as a resale guarantee.
