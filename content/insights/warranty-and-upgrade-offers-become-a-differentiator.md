**Retailers are increasingly competing on warranty and upgrade terms as much as on headline price, as buyers get better at comparing total cost of ownership.**

- A lifetime upgrade policy, letting a buyer trade a stone's value toward a larger one later, has moved from a rare perk to something buyers actively ask about.
- Warranty terms on setting repair and prong maintenance vary widely between sellers and are rarely compared as closely as stone price, despite adding real cost over time if absent.
- Buyers comparing two similar quotes are increasingly asking for warranty and upgrade terms in writing before deciding between them.
