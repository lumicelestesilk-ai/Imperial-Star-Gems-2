**A widening gap between platinum and white gold setting costs is pushing some buyers who default to platinum to reconsider white gold for a similar look.**

- Platinum is denser and typically used at higher purity than white gold, which makes it more sensitive to swings in the underlying metal price.
- White gold rhodium-plated to a bright white finish looks close to platinum new, though the plating wears and needs periodic re-application.
- Durability differences remain real regardless of price — platinum holds a prong better over decades of wear, which matters most for stones set for daily use.
