**Augmented-reality try-on features that show a ring on a buyer's own hand are shifting how online shoppers compare shapes and sizes before committing.**

- Buyers using try-on tools are reportedly more confident choosing an elongated shape like an oval or marquise than they were relying on product photography alone.
- The tools are good at showing scale and finger coverage, but they don't substitute for seeing how a stone handles light in person.
- Retailers offering try-on alongside a real-stone video, rather than either alone, are seeing the fewest post-purchase size or shape regrets.
