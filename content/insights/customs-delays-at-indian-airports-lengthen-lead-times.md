**Higher outbound parcel volumes ahead of the festive season are adding time at customs checkpoints, pushing shipping lead times out for buyers ordering close to a deadline.**

- Export clearance for polished parcels goes through additional documentation checks that take longer when volumes spike seasonally.
- The delay is measured in days rather than weeks under normal conditions, but it compounds with any other delay in the order chain.
- Orders placed with enough buffer before the festive peak avoid the worst of it; orders placed in the final week do not.
