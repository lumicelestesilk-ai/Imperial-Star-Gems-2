title: Insurance replacement values are catching up to current prices
description: Appraisals written during the price slide of the past two years are now understating replacement cost as natural prices recover.
topic: market
published: 2026-09-16
---

**Appraisals written during the past two years of falling prices are starting to understate what a stone would actually cost to replace today.**

- Replacement-value appraisals are typically a snapshot at the time of writing, and most in circulation were written while natural prices were still declining.
- With natural prices turning up at smaller sizes since the summer, some existing appraisals now sit below current market cost, which can leave a claim underpaid.
- Lab-grown appraisals face the opposite problem less often, since lab-grown prices have kept falling rather than reversing.

If a natural stone was appraised more than a year ago, it is worth asking for an updated figure before renewing a policy. We provide current replacement-value documentation with any purchase and can reappraise stones bought elsewhere — [contact us](/contact) to arrange one.
