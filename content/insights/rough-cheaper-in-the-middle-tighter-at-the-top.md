**Producers are clearing mid-size rough at lower prices while holding firm on the largest goods.**

- A leading producer's average realised rough price for the first half was about a third lower than a year earlier, and its rough price index fell 16%.
- Early in the year it cut prices on 1 to 4 ct rough and raised them on stones of 5 ct and above.
- A production pause is planned at one of its major mines for the second half.

Cheaper mid-size rough has fed through to softer prices for 1 to 2 ct natural polished over the past year, while reduced output ahead supports the larger goods.
