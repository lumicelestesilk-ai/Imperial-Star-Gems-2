**Recent auction results for fancy vivid pink and blue diamonds have landed above pre-sale estimates, and the trade is already using those prices as reference points.**

- Major auction houses report strong bidding this season for fancy vivid and fancy intense stones in pink, blue and, to a lesser extent, yellow.
- Because so few fancy colour stones of real quality change hands each year, a single strong auction result can shift what dealers quote privately for months afterward.
- The effect is concentrated at the top of the market — it has not meaningfully moved prices for the fancy yellow and pink stones most buyers actually shop for, which we [covered separately](/insights/fancy-yellow-and-pink-premiums-widen).

For buyers considering a fancy colour stone as an investment as well as a piece to wear, auction results are useful context but a poor substitute for an independent valuation on the specific stone in front of you. [Talk to the trade desk](/contact) before treating a headline auction price as a benchmark for a private purchase.
