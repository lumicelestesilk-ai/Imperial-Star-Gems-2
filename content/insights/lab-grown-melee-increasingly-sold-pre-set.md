**A growing share of lab-grown melee reaches buyers already set into pavé and halo mountings rather than sold loose, which changes how easily a price can be compared against a loose-stone quote.**

- Pre-set melee bundles stone and labour cost into a single line, which can make direct comparison against a loose-melee-plus-setting-fee quote harder than it looks at first.
- Setters working at volume have driven efficiency gains that make pre-set melee competitively priced, sometimes cheaper than sourcing loose stones and setting them separately.
- Buyers who want full visibility into what they're paying for stone versus labour should ask for the two costs broken out even on a pre-set piece.

We break out stone and setting cost separately on every quote, pre-set or otherwise. [Ask for an itemised quote](/contact) before comparing prices between suppliers.
