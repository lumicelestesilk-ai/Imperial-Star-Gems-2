**Lab-grown stones above 3 ct, hard to source a year ago, are now routinely available in most shapes.**

- Growing larger rough takes longer and has a higher failure rate, which kept 3 ct-plus polished lab-grown stones scarce and inconsistently priced through last year.
- Improvements in reactor consistency have shortened growth times for larger rough and reduced the share that fail to reach usable size or colour.
- Pricing at 3 ct and above has fallen further, in percentage terms, than at 1 ct, reflecting the improved supply.

For a large centre stone, lab-grown is now a straightforward option rather than a special order — [browse lab-grown stones above 3 ct](/lab-grown-diamonds?cmin=3.00).
