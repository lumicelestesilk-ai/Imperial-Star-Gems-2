---
title: The fluorescence discount is narrowing in top colours
description: Strong blue fluorescence used to cut deeper into D-to-F natural prices; that discount has narrowed as buyers grow more comfortable with faint haze risk.
topic: natural
published: 2026-09-16
---

**Buyers are paying closer to full price for strongly fluorescent stones in top colour grades than they did a year ago.**

- Strong or very strong blue fluorescence has traditionally carried a discount in D to F colour, on concern that it can give a stone a faint hazy look.
- That discount has narrowed as more buyers view the stone in person or on video before purchase, rather than pricing purely from the report.
- The discount remains largest at G colour and below, where fluorescence can make a stone look better than its grade, cutting the other way.

Fluorescence is worth checking in daylight before buying, not just on paper — see [fluorescence](/glossary/fluorescence) for what the grading categories mean in practice. [Ask us for a video](/contact) of any stone before you commit.
