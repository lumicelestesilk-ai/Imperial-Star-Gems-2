**Cheaper diamond simulants like moissanite and cubic zirconia are sometimes conflated with lab-grown diamond by casual buyers, despite being chemically and optically distinct materials.**

- A lab-grown diamond is chemically identical carbon, graded on the same 4Cs scale as natural; a simulant is a different material entirely, priced and graded differently.
- Confusion tends to arise from marketing language that groups all lab-created stones together loosely, without clarifying that a simulant isn't diamond at all.
- The gap matters most at resale and insurance time, when a buyer who believed they owned lab-grown diamond discovers the piece was a simulant instead.

Every stone we describe as lab-grown diamond carries a diamond grading report, never a simulant. [Check the report type](/glossary) on any stone before assuming what material you're buying.
