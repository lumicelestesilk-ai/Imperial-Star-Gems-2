**The trade show calendar running through September and October is where wholesale sentiment for the rest of the year usually firms up, and this year's early reports lean firmer.**

- Buyers returning from the first shows of the season report natural asking prices holding, with sellers less willing to negotiate on certified stones under 2 ct than they were in the spring.
- Lab-grown suppliers at the same shows were still discounting to move volume, continuing the pattern seen through the year.
- Order books written at these shows typically set supply for delivery through December, so tightness here tends to show up as longer lead times rather than higher list prices at first.

None of this is final until contracts are signed later in the season, but it lines up with the [natural price turn at smaller sizes](/insights/natural-prices-turn-from-the-small-end) we flagged last month.
