**Wholesale prices for small lab-grown melee have held roughly flat for two consecutive quarters, the first sustained pause since production volumes ramped up.**

- Melee-sized lab-grown stones, generally under 0.18 ct, have seen the steepest and most consistent price declines of any lab-grown category as production capacity grew.
- Producer margins at current prices are reportedly thin enough that further significant cuts would be difficult to sustain without a fall in production costs.
- This is a pause rather than a confirmed floor — it is one data point on top of the broader pattern of [lab-grown prices still falling, but more slowly](/insights/lab-grown-still-falling-more-slowly).

For buyers of melee-set pieces, this is a reasonable point to stop timing the market and buy against a current need, since further meaningful savings from waiting look less likely than they did a year ago.
