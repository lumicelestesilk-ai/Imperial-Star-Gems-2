**Two-stone toi et moi rings, pairing a diamond with a second stone of a contrasting shape or colour, remain a small share of orders but are growing fast from a low base.**

- The style pairs two stones of roughly similar visual weight rather than a dominant centre stone with smaller accents, which changes how the two stones need to be selected together.
- Requests most often pair a white diamond with a fancy colour natural or lab-grown stone, or with a coloured gemstone, for contrast.
- Because the two stones sit side by side with nothing to distract from either, matching cut quality between them matters more than in a traditional halo setting.
