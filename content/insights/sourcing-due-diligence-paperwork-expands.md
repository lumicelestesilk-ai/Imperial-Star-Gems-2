topic: market
published: 2026-06-25
---

**Buyers and retailers are increasingly asked for supply-chain paperwork that goes beyond the Kimberley Process certificate that has long been the baseline.**

- The Kimberley Process addresses conflict financing specifically; newer due-diligence requests cover labour standards and environmental practice at the mine and cutting-house level too.
- Larger retail chains are furthest ahead on requesting this, but the paperwork expectation is filtering down to smaller buyers as a point of comparison between suppliers.
- Suppliers who can already produce this documentation are seeing it work in their favour with retail buyers doing supplier reviews.
