topic: market
published: 2026-09-20

**A short 360-degree video of the actual stone, not a stock image of the same specification, is turning from a nice-to-have into an expected part of the sale.**

- A grading report confirms the specification; it says nothing about how a particular stone actually looks moving under light, which is what a video shows.
- The shift has been driven mainly by online buying, where a video is the closest substitute for handling the stone in person before committing.
- Some buyers now ask for video even on in-person purchases, treating it as a permanent record of the exact stone bought rather than a sales aid.
