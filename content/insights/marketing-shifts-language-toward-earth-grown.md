**Industry marketing campaigns are increasingly using earth-grown or earth-mined rather than simply natural, a wording shift aimed at distinguishing more clearly from lab-grown in buyers' minds.**

- The terminology shift responds to buyer confusion in some markets about whether natural and lab-grown stones are simply graded differently, rather than being fundamentally different in origin.
- Earth-grown emphasises geological origin and time scale, a contrast marketers see as more evocative than the more clinical natural versus lab-grown split.
- The change is cosmetic rather than regulatory — grading reports still use natural and laboratory-grown as the formal terms regardless of marketing language.

Whatever the marketing term, the report is what defines a stone — we always list origin plainly and match the marketing language to the formal grading terms. [See how we label stones](/glossary) in our own listings.
