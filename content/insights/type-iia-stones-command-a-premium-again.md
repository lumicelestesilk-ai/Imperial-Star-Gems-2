**Type IIa rough has thinned out, and polished Type IIa is pricing above ordinary colourless stones of the same grade.**

- Cutters report fewer parcels yielding Type IIa rough, the chemically purest class of natural diamond, prized for its exceptional transparency.
- Polished Type IIa stones in D to F colour are now pricing several points above ordinary stones of the same colour and clarity.
- The premium is most visible from 1 ct upward, where a grading report noting the classification adds resale confidence.

Type IIa is a classification, not a grade, so it sits alongside colour and clarity rather than replacing them — see [colour grade](/glossary/color-grade) and [grading report](/glossary/grading-report) for how the two interact. Buyers chasing maximum transparency at a given size should ask specifically whether a stone has been tested for it. [Browse natural stock](/natural-diamonds).
