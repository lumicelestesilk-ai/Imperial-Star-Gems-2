**Buyers are increasingly asking which grower produced a lab-grown stone, not just which lab graded it, as a small number of growers build reputations distinct from the labs themselves.**

- A grading report confirms specification and origin as lab-grown, but doesn't typically name the specific grower, which some buyers now want to know regardless.
- A handful of growers have built consumer-facing brand recognition through consistent quality and marketing, similar to how some natural mine origins carry their own reputation.
- This is still a minority of buyers, but it's a newer behaviour than simply comparing specification and price across otherwise interchangeable lab-grown stock.
