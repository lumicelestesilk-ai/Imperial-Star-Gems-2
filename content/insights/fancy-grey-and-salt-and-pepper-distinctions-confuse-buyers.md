**Buyers filtering natural stones by standard clarity grades sometimes miss fancy grey and salt-and-pepper stones entirely, since both sit outside the usual clarity-driven pricing logic.**

- Fancy grey stones get their colour from trace elements and can carry a standard clarity grade; salt-and-pepper stones are graded more on the visual pattern of their inclusions than a conventional scale.
- A search filtered strictly by clarity grade can exclude salt-and-pepper stones that would otherwise suit a buyer looking for something unconventional.
- The two are sometimes confused with each other despite being different categories, which is worth clarifying before assuming a stone's price reflects a standard clarity grade.
