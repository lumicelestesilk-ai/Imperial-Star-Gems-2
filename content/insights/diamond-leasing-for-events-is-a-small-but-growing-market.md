**Short-term rental of loose diamonds and set jewellery for one-off events remains a small business, but the number of buyers asking about it has increased this year.**

- Leasing arrangements typically cover a fixed period — an event weekend, a photo shoot, a single occasion — with the stone insured for the duration and returned afterward.
- The model works best for larger or fancy colour stones, where the cost of buying outright for a single occasion is hard to justify against renting.
- It remains a small part of overall trade volume, but enquiries have grown faster than the broader market this year, largely from event stylists and one-off private occasions rather than ongoing retail demand.
