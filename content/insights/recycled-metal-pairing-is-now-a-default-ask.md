**Buyers weighing natural against lab-grown stones are now routinely asking whether the setting metal is recycled too, treating it as part of the same overall decision.**

- Recycled gold and platinum, refined from existing jewellery and industrial scrap rather than newly mined ore, is now widely available at little to no cost premium.
- The question has shifted from whether recycled metal is available to whether a specific supplier defaults to it or charges extra for it.
- This mirrors the broader pattern of buyers wanting full sourcing information across the whole piece, not just the centre stone.
