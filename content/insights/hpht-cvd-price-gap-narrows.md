**HPHT and CVD lab-grown stones, once priced apart, are now trading within a point or two of each other at the same grade.**

- CVD, the more common growth method for larger colourless stones, has historically priced slightly above HPHT at equivalent colour and clarity.
- As HPHT growers have improved colour consistency at 1 ct and above, the gap between the two methods has closed for most standard sizes.
- HPHT retains a price edge specifically in fancy vivid colours, where its growth process still yields stronger, more even colour.

For a colourless stone, growth method now matters less than the grading report itself — see [brilliant cut](/glossary/brilliant-cut) and [grading report](/glossary/grading-report) for what to check instead. Growth method still matters for fancy colour buyers: [ask us](/contact) which method produced a specific coloured stone before you buy.
