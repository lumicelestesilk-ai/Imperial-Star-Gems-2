**Buyers assembling stacks of several thin bands, often bought over time rather than all at once, are creating steady demand for consistently matched small stones.**

- A stack is typically built piece by piece, which means a later band needs to match the colour and cut style of earlier ones bought months or years before.
- This has made consistent house standards for melee colour and cut more valuable than any single flashy stone, since mismatched sparkle is the most common complaint.
- Lab-grown melee's price stability has made it a popular choice for stacks specifically because it's easier to match consistently over repeat purchases.
