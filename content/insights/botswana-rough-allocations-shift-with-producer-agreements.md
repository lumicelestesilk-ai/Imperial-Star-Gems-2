**Renegotiated agreements between Botswana and major rough producers are changing how rough is allocated to sightholders, with knock-on effects further down the cutting chain.**

- Botswana has been pushing for a larger share of rough to be cut and polished domestically rather than exported for cutting elsewhere.
- This shifts some cutting volume away from traditional centres and adds a domestic beneficiation step that can affect turnaround for certain rough categories.
- The practical effect for buyers so far has been on timing and sourcing paperwork rather than on finished-stone price.
