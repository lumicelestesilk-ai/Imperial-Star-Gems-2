**Sellers who go straight from cutter to customer, skipping the traditional wholesale-to-retail chain, are putting real pressure on jewellers carrying higher overhead.**

- A traditional path from cutter to wholesaler to retailer to customer typically adds a markup at each stage; cutting out a stage lowers the final price without necessarily lowering quality.
- Buyers increasingly compare a direct-to-consumer quote against a showroom quote for the same specification before deciding where to buy.
- Showrooms still win on in-person comparison and same-day handling, which direct sellers can't easily replicate.
