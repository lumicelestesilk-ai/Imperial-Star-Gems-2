**Most lab-grown parcels now arrive with an IGI report rather than a GIA one; natural stones remain mostly GIA.**

- Across our recent lab-grown intake, IGI reports outnumber GIA reports by a wide margin.
- Natural stones show the opposite pattern: GIA remains the more common report by far.
- Turnaround time is the main driver — IGI has expanded lab-grown capacity faster than demand has grown, keeping wait times short.

The two labs use the same grading scales, so a VS1 from one reads the same as a VS1 from the other; see [grading report](/glossary/grading-report), [GIA](/glossary/gia) and [IGI](/glossary/igi) for what each report actually certifies. What differs is resale familiarity: buyers researching lab-grown are now just as used to seeing IGI. [See lab-grown stones in stock](/lab-grown-diamonds).
