**Natural diamonds mined and often cut in Canada continue to command a modest, steady premium among buyers who prioritise a specific, verifiable country of origin.**

- Canadian mines operate under environmental and labour standards that some buyers specifically value, and stones are often laser-inscribed with a polar bear or maple leaf logo to mark origin.
- The premium has stayed fairly stable over the past few years, unlike some other provenance premiums that have moved with broader sourcing trends.
- Supply is naturally limited by mine output, which keeps Canadian-origin stones a smaller category rather than a mainstream alternative to unspecified origin.
