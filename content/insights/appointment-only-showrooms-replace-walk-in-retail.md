**More sellers of higher-value stones are moving to an appointment-only showroom model, citing better security and more focused buyer attention than open walk-in retail.**

- An appointment model lets a seller prepare specific stones in advance based on what a buyer has said they're looking for, rather than showing whatever happens to be on display.
- Security is a real driver too — fewer walk-in visitors means less exposure for high-value inventory kept on site.
- The trade-off is spontaneity: a buyer wanting to browse casually is better served by a traditional retail floor than a scheduled appointment.
