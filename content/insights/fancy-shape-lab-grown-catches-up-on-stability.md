**Fancy-shape lab-grown stones are stabilising, closing a gap that opened last year when rounds settled first.**

- Lab-grown rounds found a floor earlier this year; fancy shapes kept sliding for several months afterward.
- Over the summer, month-on-month declines in lab-grown ovals, cushions and emerald cuts narrowed sharply, following the pattern rounds set earlier.
- Emerald and radiant cuts, which use more rough per carat of finished stone, have firmed the most among fancies.

For buyers who prefer a fancy shape but held off waiting for lab-grown prices to bottom, the gap to rounds is closing rather than widening further. Make still separates well-cut fancies from the rest — the [cut guide](/cut-guide) and individual [shape pages](/shapes) cover proportions to check before price.
