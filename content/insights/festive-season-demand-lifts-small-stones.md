**The run-up to Diwali and the autumn wedding season is showing up first in stones under a carat.**

- Retail orders placed with us over the past month have skewed toward 0.30 ct to 0.70 ct stones, both natural and lab-grown.
- Cutters are prioritising these sizes, which has tightened supply and firmed asking prices faster than at 1 ct and above.
- Matched pairs for earrings have been the single busiest request category.

If a purchase can wait until after the festive peak, larger stones and one-off fancy shapes are seeing less pressure and more room to negotiate.
