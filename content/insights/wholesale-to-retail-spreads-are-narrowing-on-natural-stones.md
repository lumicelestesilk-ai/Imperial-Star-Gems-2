**As wholesale natural prices firm at smaller sizes, the gap between wholesale cost and typical retail asking price has narrowed from where it sat during the two-year slide.**

- During the period of falling natural prices, retail asking prices adjusted more slowly than wholesale, which widened margins for sellers holding older stock.
- With [natural prices turning up from the small end](/insights/natural-prices-turn-from-the-small-end), that gap is closing as wholesale cost catches up to, and in some cases overtakes, retail prices set before the turn.
- Sellers still pricing off stock bought during the slide are the most exposed, and some are re-pricing older inventory upward to protect margin.

For buyers, this means older retail listings advertised as discounted may no longer represent as large a saving against current wholesale cost as they did earlier in the year.
