**Vintage-style settings have pushed demand for antique cushion and old European cut stones well ahead of what the market can supply.**

- Requests for rings styled after early-1900s settings have risen sharply this year, and most of those designs call for a soft, low-brilliance cut rather than a modern round.
- Genuine old European cuts, cut by hand before modern proportions were standardised, are scarce; most of what fills demand is a modern cut reproducing the look.
- Reproduction antique cushions in good make are pricing close to standard cushions, while genuine antique-cut stones carry a real scarcity premium.
