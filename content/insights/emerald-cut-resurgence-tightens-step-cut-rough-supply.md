**Renewed demand for emerald cut stones is tightening supply of rough with the proportions needed to yield a well-balanced step cut without excessive weight loss.**

- Step cuts show inclusions more readily than brilliant cuts, so cutters need cleaner rough to begin with, which narrows the pool of suitable material.
- Emerald cuts also require careful proportioning to avoid a washed-out, low-fire look, which takes more cutting expertise and time than a standard round.
- The combined effect is longer lead times on custom emerald cut orders relative to rounds and cushions cut from more widely available rough.
