**Retailers who moved away from layaway in recent years are reintroducing structured instalment plans specifically for larger stone purchases.**

- A traditional layaway plan holds a stone against staged payments before it changes hands; newer instalment plans more often transfer the stone at purchase with financing behind it.
- The reintroduction tracks the same cautious spending pattern showing up elsewhere in discretionary retail, not anything specific to diamonds.
- Terms vary widely between providers, and buyers should check whether interest applies and what happens if a payment is missed before committing to a plan.
