**More retailers are offering to take a lab-grown stone back against a future upgrade, as falling prices make older purchases look expensive by comparison.**

- Because lab-grown prices have kept falling, a stone bought a few years ago is typically worth well under its original purchase price today, which has made straightforward resale a hard sell for owners.
- Trade-in programs address this by letting a buyer put the stone's current market value toward a larger or better stone, rather than trying to sell it outright at a loss.
- The trade-in value offered is based on current wholesale price for that specification, not the original receipt — buyers going in expecting to recover close to what they paid are often disappointed.
