**As yellow gold settings regain popularity after years of white metal dominance, buyer interest is shifting toward warmer colour-grade stones that suit the tone better than icy colourless ones.**

- A colourless D-to-F stone can look slightly cold set in yellow gold, an effect some buyers now actively want to avoid by choosing a warmer colour grade instead.
- G-to-J colour stones, once considered a compromise, are increasingly chosen deliberately for how well they read against yellow metal.
- The shift has helped narrow demand-driven pricing gaps between top and mid colour grades in shapes commonly set in yellow gold, like cushions and ovals.

If you've chosen yellow gold, it's worth seeing a warmer colour grade against it before assuming a top grade is necessary. [Build a ring](/build-a-ring) to compare metal and colour combinations directly.
