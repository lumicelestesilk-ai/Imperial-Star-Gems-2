**First-time UK buyers importing a loose stone from overseas are sometimes surprised by VAT due on arrival, which isn't always folded into the quoted purchase price.**

- A quote from an overseas seller may be for the stone alone, with import VAT and any handling fee added separately once the parcel reaches the UK.
- The rate and handling can vary by courier and declared value, which makes the landed cost harder to predict from the sale price alone.
- Buyers comparing an overseas quote against a UK-based seller should compare landed cost, not sticker price, to make a fair comparison.
