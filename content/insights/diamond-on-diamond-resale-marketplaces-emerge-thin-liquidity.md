**New marketplaces built specifically for reselling lab-grown diamonds between private owners have launched, though trading volume and price discovery remain thin compared to established secondhand markets.**

- A resale marketplace depends on enough listings and buyers to set a reliable going rate, and lab-grown resale hasn't yet reached that density.
- Sellers listing on these platforms often end up pricing well above what a dealer would offer directly, since a private buyer willing to pay closer to retail is the target market.
- This ties into the same dynamic behind [growing trade-in programs](/insights/lab-grown-trade-in-programs-are-becoming-more-common) — trading in against an upgrade is currently a more reliable route to value than a private resale listing.
