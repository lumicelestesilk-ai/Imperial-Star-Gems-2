**Enquiries for three-stone settings, pairing a centre stone with two side stones, have picked up after several years of single-stone solitaires dominating custom requests.**

- The style is often marketed around past-present-future symbolism, but the practical driver we see is buyers wanting more visible sparkle without increasing centre stone size.
- Matching side stones to a centre stone in colour and cut adds a sourcing step that a single-stone solitaire doesn't require.
- Trapezoid and half-moon side stones are the most requested shapes to flank a round or cushion centre stone.
