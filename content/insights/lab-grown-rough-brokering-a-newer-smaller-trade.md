**Trading lab-grown rough between growers and cutters, rather than finished polished stones, remains a much smaller and newer trade than the well-established polished brokering business.**

- Many growers cut their own rough in-house rather than selling it on, which limits the pool of rough actually available to independent brokers compared to the natural rough trade.
- Where rough does trade independently, pricing is closely tied to growth method and reactor run quality, factors a broker has to assess without centuries of established grading convention to lean on.
- The trade is expected to grow as more independent cutting capacity comes online specifically to serve growers who don't cut their own production.
