**Top colour and clarity cost far more in natural stones than in lab-grown, so the best value sits in different grades for each.**

- In natural stones, price climbs most steeply across the colourless grades, D to F, and at the highest clarities.
- Lab-grown production yields high clarity routinely: nearly nine in ten of our lab-grown stones grade VS1 or VVS2.
- Most of our lab-grown stock is fancy coloured; the stones on the colourless scale are all D to F.

In natural, G to H colour and an eye-clean VS2 to SI1 is where a budget goes furthest. In lab-grown, high grades come as standard, so choose on cut and size. The [colour guide](/color-guide) and [clarity guide](/clarity-guide) set out how each grade reads in practice.
