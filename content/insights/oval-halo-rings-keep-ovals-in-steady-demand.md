**The enduring popularity of oval halo engagement rings keeps oval natural diamonds in steady demand, even though ovals are more prone to a visible bulge at the widest point than other elongated shapes.**

- A bulge, or bow-tie effect, shows up as a darker band across the stone's centre and is more common in ovals than in most other elongated cuts.
- A halo setting can help disguise a mild bulge by drawing the eye to the surrounding smaller stones, which is part of why the pairing has stayed popular.
- Buyers focused specifically on avoiding bow-tie effect should ask to see the actual stone on video rather than judging from a certificate alone, since proportions on paper don't fully predict it.
