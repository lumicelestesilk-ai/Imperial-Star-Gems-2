**For US trade buyers, the tariff position has settled, though not where it was expected to.**

- Early in the year, the US and India agreed a framework that would remove duty on India-cut natural diamonds.
- That access depends on a wider trade agreement that has not yet come into force.
- For now, India-polished diamonds entering the US carry a 10% duty, while stones polished in Belgium, Botswana and Namibia are exempt.

Clarity alone has helped: trading out of India picked up after the summer slowdown. US buyers should price the duty into India-polished goods and watch for zero-duty access taking effect, which would lower landed cost on natural stones.
