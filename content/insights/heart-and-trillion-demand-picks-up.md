**Heart and trillion shapes, long a niche corner of fancy-shape demand, have picked up noticeably in orders this year.**

- Both shapes have moved from occasional special orders to a regular share of fancy-shape enquiries, mostly for anniversary and statement pieces rather than engagement rings.
- Well-cut trillions, used mainly as side stones, are in tighter supply than mediocre ones, and the price gap between the two has widened.
- Hearts remain the more demanding shape to cut symmetrically, and buyers are increasingly asking to see the stone rather than buying from the report alone.

For a side-stone trillion, symmetry matters more than for almost any other shape — see [symmetry](/glossary/symmetry) and the [trillion](/shapes/trillion) and [heart](/shapes/heart) shape pages. The [anniversary and milestone stones guide](/guides/anniversary-and-milestone-stones) covers how these shapes are typically used.
