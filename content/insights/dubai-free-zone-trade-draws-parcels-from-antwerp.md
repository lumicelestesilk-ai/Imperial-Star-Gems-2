**Lower transaction costs and faster logistics through Dubai's diamond free zone are pulling a growing share of parcel trading away from the traditional Antwerp route.**

- Free zone status in Dubai removes some of the duty and tax friction that adds cost to trading through certain other hubs.
- Flight connectivity between India, the Gulf and onward markets has made Dubai a practical staging point for parcels that would once have routed through Antwerp.
- Antwerp remains dominant for high-value single stones and specialist expertise, while Dubai has grown faster in mixed commercial-grade parcel volume.
