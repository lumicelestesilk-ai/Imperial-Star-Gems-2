topic: market

**Requests for chain-of-custody paperwork alongside the grading report are no longer limited to large or unusual stones — they are becoming routine on everyday purchases.**

- A grading report describes the stone; it does not on its own show where the rough was mined or cut, which is a separate question buyers are now asking more often.
- The shift has been driven partly by retail jewellers marketing traceability as a feature, and partly by buyers who simply want a fuller paper trail before paying.
- Supplying this adds a step to the sale but rarely changes the price — it has mostly changed what buyers expect to receive alongside the certificate.
