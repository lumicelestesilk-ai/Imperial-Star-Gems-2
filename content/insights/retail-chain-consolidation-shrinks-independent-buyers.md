**A wave of acquisitions among mid-size jewellery retail chains has reduced the number of genuinely independent buying decisions being made at the wholesale counter.**

- Consolidated chains increasingly buy centrally for multiple store locations, replacing what used to be several separate purchasing relationships.
- This concentrates wholesale buying power in fewer hands, which can mean better terms for large chains and less flexibility for smaller, independent showrooms.
- Independent jewellers who buy in smaller volumes are leaning more on relationships with suppliers who still deal in single-stone and small-lot quantities.
