**Supply of natural pink diamonds has tightened further as production from the mines historically responsible for most global pink output continues its long decline.**

- A handful of now-depleted or closed mines accounted for the large majority of natural pink diamonds ever brought to market, and nothing has replaced that volume since.
- New pink diamond finds from other locations remain sporadic and small by comparison, which keeps the category structurally short of demand.
- This scarcity sits behind the [widening fancy pink and yellow premiums](/insights/fancy-yellow-and-pink-premiums-widen) we've flagged before, with pink specifically tightening faster than yellow.
