**HPHT lab-grown production is concentrated among a smaller number of larger operations than CVD, reflecting the higher capital cost of HPHT presses relative to CVD reactors.**

- HPHT presses are expensive, large pieces of equipment that favour operators able to run them at scale, which naturally limits the number of viable producers.
- CVD reactors are comparatively cheaper to add incrementally, which is part of why CVD production has spread across a larger number of smaller growers.
- This structural difference sits behind the [narrowing HPHT-to-CVD price gap](/insights/hpht-cvd-price-gap-narrows) in a different way than pure production cost — fewer HPHT producers means less price competition pushing that method's prices down as fast.
