**Cutting houses are booking fuller through the fourth quarter, and turnaround on custom orders is starting to stretch.**

- Order books at the cutting and polishing houses we work with are fuller than at this point last year, for both natural and lab-grown rough.
- Standard shapes and sizes are largely unaffected, but bespoke cuts and non-standard proportions are taking longer to schedule.
- The tightening tracks the same festive and wedding-season demand pushing up prices at smaller sizes.
