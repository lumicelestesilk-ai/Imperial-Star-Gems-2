**A reshuffled trade show calendar has moved some autumn buying activity that traditionally ran through Hong Kong toward Bangkok instead.**

- Hong Kong has long anchored the September show season for coloured stones and, to a lesser extent, diamonds sold into Asian retail.
- Venue and scheduling changes this cycle pushed several exhibitors to add or shift to a Bangkok date, splitting attendance between the two.
- For buyers, this has meant planning two trips instead of one, or picking a single show and accepting a narrower supplier list.
