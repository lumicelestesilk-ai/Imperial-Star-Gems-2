**The fall in lab-grown prices is slowing, and it now depends heavily on size.**

- Wholesale prices in the second quarter were about 13% lower than a year earlier, a slightly smaller fall than the quarter before.
- One-carat rounds edged up over the same period.
- Stones of 1.50 to 1.99 ct fell about 11%, and 2 ct stones about 20%.

At a carat, lab-grown pricing appears to be finding a floor, which makes it a reasonable point to buy. Above 1.5 ct prices are still adjusting: buy for the stone rather than its resale value, and ask for a price on the day.
